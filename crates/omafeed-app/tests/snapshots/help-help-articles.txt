List articles, newest first

Usage: omafeed articles [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --scope <SCOPE>    unread, today, starred, all, feed:ID or folder:ID [default: unread]
      --search <SEARCH>  Search within the scope, as `search` does
      --exact            Disable typo, plural and prefix expansion
      --unread           Only unread articles
      --since <SINCE>    Only articles published this recently, e.g. 90m, 24h, 2d or 1w
      --limit <LIMIT>    [default: 50]
      --offset <OFFSET>  [default: 0]
      --json             Print JSON, with a preview of each article, instead of text
  -h, --help             Print help
