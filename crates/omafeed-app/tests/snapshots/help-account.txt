Connect a Miniflux account, show it, or disconnect it

Usage: omafeed account <COMMAND>

Commands:
  connect     Mirror a Miniflux account, reading its API key from standard input
  status      Show the connected account and its last sync
  disconnect  Stop mirroring the account; its feeds stay, as local ones
  help        Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

  -h, --help  Print help
