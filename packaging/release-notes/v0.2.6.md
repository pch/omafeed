**TL;DR:** omafeed 0.2.6 makes the window simpler to work in. You organize feeds and folders right in the sidebar by dragging them, instead of in a separate "Manage library" window. Articles get a right-click menu. Single feeds and folders can be refreshed on their own. The dialogs have a new look, and the status bar is gone. Your library and settings carry over unchanged.

## New

- **A right-click menu on articles** ([#36](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/36)), as in NetNewsWire. From it you can mark an article read or unread, star or unstar it, mark the articles above or below it as read (Ctrl+Z undoes this), open it in the browser, copy its link, or go to its feed. The Menu key or Shift+F10 opens the menu for the selected article.
- **Refresh a single feed or folder** from its right-click menu ([#35](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/35)). Use it, for instance, to retry a feed marked unavailable without refreshing everything. Adding or editing a feed now refreshes only that feed.
- **A companion Omarchy bar plugin** ([#37](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/37)). [omafeed-bar](https://github.com/pch/omafeed-bar) puts an RSS icon in the Omarchy bar that lights up when something is unread, with the newest articles one click away. Install it with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/pch/omafeed-bar --enable`.

## Changed

- **No more "Manage library" window** ([#35](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/35)). Feeds and folders are organized right in the sidebar, as in NetNewsWire. Drag a feed or folder onto a folder to move it inside, or onto a smart view to move it to the top level. The **+** button in the header adds a feed (Ctrl+N) or a folder (Ctrl+Shift+N) to the folder you are viewing, and a folder's right-click menu can add them inside it. **Edit…** and **Remove…** stay in the right-click menu. Ctrl+L no longer does anything.
- **Redesigned dialogs** ([#35](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/35), [#36](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/36)). Adding and editing feeds and folders, choosing among a site's feeds, Settings, Content filters and Keyboard shortcuts all have a new look in omafeed's own style: a clear title, labeled fields, Cancel and Save at the bottom, and Enter to save. A mistake, such as a folder name already in use, shows in the form instead of closing it. Content filters is now a dialog rather than a separate window.
- **No status bar** ([#35](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/35)). Refresh progress shows as a spinner in the refresh button and as a thin line along the top of the window. The button's tooltip shows how far a refresh has got and when the last one finished. The page buttons sit beside the article count and only appear when a view has more than one page.
- **The sidebar no longer has a "LIBRARY" heading** ([#36](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/36)). Folders have a folder icon, and their expander is at the end of the row.
- **A quieter header and a lowercase name** ([#36](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/36)). The title is a small "omafeed" wordmark, and the app is called omafeed everywhere, including the launcher.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.2.5. The `.deb` packages are for Debian 13 or newer and Ubuntu 26.04 or newer. The `.rpm` packages are for Fedora 43 or newer. The Arch package is x86_64; on ARM, build the `PKGBUILD`. The archives need glibc 2.34 or newer, `gtk4`, `libadwaita` 1.6 or newer and `webkitgtk-6.0`.
