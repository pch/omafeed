**TL;DR:** Omafeed 0.1.2 is a small fix: switching to another feed, folder or view now closes the open article instead of leaving the previous view's article on screen.

**Download:** [Arch Linux recipe](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.1.2/PKGBUILD) (`makepkg -si`) · Linux archive ([x86_64](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.1.2/omafeed-0.1.2-x86_64-linux.tar.gz))

## Fixed

- **Switching views clears the reader.** Selecting a different feed, folder or smart view used to keep showing the previous view's article, unselected in the new list. The reader now closes, and an article still loading is dropped. Refreshes, search, the unread filter and paging keep the article open.

## Requirements

Omafeed needs `gtk4`, `libadwaita` 1.6 or newer and `webkitgtk-6.0`. The x86_64 archive is built on Arch Linux.
