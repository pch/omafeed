**TL;DR:** Omafeed 0.2.1 is a maintenance release that updates several libraries Omafeed is built on, including its feed parser. It adds no features, and your library is unchanged.

**Download:** [Arch Linux recipe](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.2.1/PKGBUILD) (`makepkg -si`) · Linux archive ([x86_64](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.2.1/omafeed-0.2.1-x86_64-linux.tar.gz))

## Changed

- **Updated libraries.** feed-rs 3.0 (the feed parser), toml 1.1, quick-xml 0.42, sha2 0.11 and base64 0.23 ([#1](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/1), [#2](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/2), [#3](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/3), [#4](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/4), [#7](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/7)). Article IDs and cached icon names are unchanged by the update, so existing articles are not duplicated and icons are not re-fetched.

## Requirements

Omafeed needs `gtk4`, `libadwaita` 1.6 or newer and `webkitgtk-6.0`. The x86_64 archive is built on Arch Linux.
