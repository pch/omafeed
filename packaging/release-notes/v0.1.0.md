**TL;DR:** Omafeed 0.1.0 is the first release: a native RSS reader for Linux and Omarchy with a three-pane library, article list and reading view. Your subscriptions and articles stay on your computer, with no account, telemetry or cloud sync.

**Download:** [Arch Linux recipe](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.1.0/PKGBUILD) (`makepkg -si`) · Linux archive ([x86_64](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/releases/download/v0.1.0/omafeed-0.1.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz))

## New

- **Reads RSS, Atom and JSON Feed.** Refresh by hand or on a schedule while the app is open. Requests use conditional HTTP, bounded concurrency, timeouts and retry backoff, and each feed shows its own error when it fails.
- **Subscribe from a website or feed URL.** Omafeed discovers the site's feeds, lets you choose when there are several, and never saves an invalid page as a subscription. The feed's URL can be corrected later without losing saved articles.
- **Nested folders.** Create, rename, move and remove folders, and move feeds between them without losing article history or read state. Removing a folder promotes its feeds and subfolders to its parent.
- **OPML import and export** with duplicate detection and folder hierarchy preserved. Import from the menu or with `omafeed import FILE`.
- **Views and search.** All Unread, Today, Starred, All Articles, folder and feed views, with full-text search across the current view (nested folders included) and an unread filter.
- **Read state, stars and offline text.** Mark articles read or unread and star them. Mark the current view read with Ctrl+Shift+A and undo it with Ctrl+Z. Article text works offline.
- **A reader for sanitized HTML** with images, code blocks, adjustable typography, copy link and open original. Article JavaScript, frames, forms and embedded media are disabled, and remote images can be turned off in Settings.
- **Omarchy theme following.** The top bar, menus, sidebar and reader take their colors from the current Omarchy theme and update when it changes. Light and dark modes, remembered window and pane sizes, favicon caching and keyboard navigation (press ? for the shortcuts) are included.

## Requirements

Omafeed needs `gtk4`, `libadwaita` 1.6 or newer and `webkitgtk-6.0`. The x86_64 archive is built on Arch Linux.
