## Fixed

- **Feeds on cbc.ca, and other sites behind Akamai's bot filtering, load again** ([#38](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/issues/38), [#39](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/39)). Before, subscribing to or refreshing them gave up with "operation timed out" after 30 seconds. The filter never answered requests whose user agent mentioned GitHub, as omafeed's did. omafeed now identifies itself only as `omafeed/<version>`.
- **Arabic, Hebrew and other right-to-left articles read right to left** ([#40](https://github.com/pch/omafeed/pull/40)). Before, the reader laid every article out left to right, so right-to-left text was left-aligned and quotes had their bar on the wrong side. The title, each paragraph, heading, list item and quote now follow the direction of their own text, so an article that mixes languages lines up too. Code blocks stay left to right. Right-to-left text uses Noto Sans Arabic first, because WebKitGTK misplaces Arabic diacritics when it falls back to another font.

## Changed

- **A new app icon**: a frosted card with the feed symbol over a coral-to-violet gradient.

## Requirements

Unchanged from 0.2.6. The `.deb` packages are for Debian 13 or newer and Ubuntu 26.04 or newer. The `.rpm` packages are for Fedora 43 or newer. The Arch package is x86_64; on ARM, build the `PKGBUILD`. The archives need glibc 2.34 or newer, `gtk4`, `libadwaita` 1.6 or newer and `webkitgtk-6.0`.
